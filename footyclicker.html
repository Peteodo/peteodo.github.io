Footy Clicker - Road to Glory

Take a club from Sunday League all the way to the Club World Cup.
The goal
Win your league for a trophy — top 2 go up automatically, 3rd gets a shot via the play-offs. But promotion also needs enough cash banked to meet the next tier's threshold. Fall short on either and you stay put.
Two ways to invest
Business upgrades build your income — tickets, sponsors, the stadium. Squad upgrades build your on-pitch quality — players, academy, coaching. The big click button switches between Commercial and Squad focus, so you choose where your attention goes.
Your Manager
Every manager has a hidden skill rating that quietly shifts how your team performs. Sack them any time for a fresh, unknown roll — but fans punish snap decisions, especially when things were going well.
Training Ground
Squad Skill has a hard cap until you expand this. No amount of clicking gets you past it — invest in both sides, not just one.
