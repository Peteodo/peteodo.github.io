Club Grounds: NFL Edition

Build a football franchise from a local semi-pro team all the way to a Super Bowl champion.
The objective
Finish in the top 2 of your league AND bank enough cash to move up a status (3rd place earns a playoff game with a chance of going up). Winning alone isn’t enough: build a franchise that can afford the next level. Reach the top and you can bid for an NFL expansion franchise.
Walking the grounds
Your head coach walks the map. Use the arrow keys or WASD, or tap anywhere (tap a building to walk straight to its door). Stand still at a door and its upgrades pop up, with a full explanation of what the building does under the map. Buildings pulse when you can afford something there.
Making money
Stand on the gold $ mat at the Ticket Office to earn cash; Space or tapping the mat speeds it up. Business buildings are on the left: marketing and merchandise, sponsors, season tickets and seat licenses, and specialists at the Front Office.
Building the team
The green mat at the Practice Facility earns practice reps that turn into Team Rating, up to the cap set by the facility. Football buildings are on the right: the Draft Room, Free Agency and scouting.
Game days
Ten games a season are played in the stadium, with the standings above it. Fan mood and fanbase grow with results, and the crowd lets you know exactly what it thinks.
Going pro
Once you earn an NFL expansion franchise, you join a 32-team league with two conferences and four-team divisions. Win your division or grab a wild card to reach the playoffs: Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Championship and the Super Bowl. The top seed in each conference gets a bye, and every playoff round pays revenue. Win the Super Bowl to complete the legacy.
Cap and draft
In the NFL a salary cap limits your payroll: spend over it and you pay a 50% luxury tax on the excess. Each year the draft hands the worst records the first picks. Rookies improve your team now and even more after 3 seasons, and a rare pick can turn out to be a Franchise Quarterback.
Coming soon
You start with just the Ticket Office, Free Agency, the Practice Facility and the Stadium. Plots wrapped in yellow and black tape are coming soon: stand at one to see what it will do and when it opens.
Your head coach
Stand at the stadium door to see your head coach. Every head coach has a hidden skill that is revealed over a few seasons, and their schemes get predictable the longer they stay. Fire them any time and pick a replacement, but fans punish snap decisions.
The Sideline
Stand at the stadium door and a Go to Sideline button appears under your head coach. Watch the game from the sideline: your presence will impact the result.
