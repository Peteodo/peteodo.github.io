Club Grounds: Road to Glory

Take a club from Sunday League all the way to the Club World Cup.
The goal
Finish in the top 2 of your league AND bank enough cash to move up (3rd place gets a play-off with a chance of going up). Winning alone isn’t enough, so build a club that can afford the next level.
Walking the grounds
Your manager walks the map. Use the arrow keys or WASD, or tap anywhere (tap a building to walk straight to its door). Stand still at a door and its upgrades pop up. Buildings pulse when you can afford something there.
Making money
Stand on the gold £ mat at the Ticket Office to earn cash; Space or tapping the mat speeds it up. Business upgrades are on the left: marketing, sponsors, season tickets, and consultants at the Club Offices.
Building the squad
The teal mat at the Training Ground makes training points that turn into Squad Skill, up to the cap set by the Training Ground. Squad buildings are on the right: academy, transfers and recruitment.
Match days
Ten matches a season are played in the stadium, with the league table above it. Fan mood and fanbase grow with results.
Coming soon
You start with just the Ticket Office, Transfers, the Training Ground and the Stadium. Plots wrapped in yellow and black tape are coming soon: new buildings and upgrades are revealed as your club grows.
Your manager
Stand at the stadium door to see your manager. Every manager has a hidden skill that is revealed over a few seasons, and they go stale the longer they stay. Sack them any time and pick a replacement, but fans punish snap decisions.
